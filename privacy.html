Privacy policy / Politique de confidentialité

Last updated / Mise à jour : 2026-11-15

English

Pocket Card Shop is a card-shop management and dueling game. We run no server: we create no account and receive no personal data, except what you choose to send us (e-mail, bug report). The game only relies on the Google services described below. Contact: freetimaplay@gmail.com.

Data

  • Your game (shop, collection, decks, settings) — on your phone, and in your Google account's Android backup if enabled.
  • Google Play Games player ID, saved game, achievements, leaderboard scores — to get your game back on another phone, achievements and leaderboards (when you are signed in to Google Play Games).
  • In-app purchase order information — payment and restoring purchases (Google Play).
  • Advertising ID and diagnostics — ads, according to your consent (Google AdMob).
  • Review request — the official “Rate this game” window (Google Play).
  • Bug report (only if you choose to send it): technical log, device model and your saved game without purchase data — sent by you, by e-mail, to support, to fix the reported problem; deleted once handled.

Your Play Games nickname and scores are visible to other players according to your Play Games profile settings. We have no access to your e-mail address.

Ads and consent

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, consent is requested at first launch (Google form) and can be changed at any time in Settings → “Ad choices”. Rewarded ads are always optional. The “Remove ads” purchase removes interstitial ads.

Sharing

We never sell or receive data. Only the Google services above process what they need, under Google's privacy policy.

Deletion

See Delete my data.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over.

Your rights (GDPR)

Any question: freetimaplay@gmail.com.
